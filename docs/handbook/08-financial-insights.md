# Financial Insights

> **The question this page answers:** "How did my month go, and what changed?"

## 1. At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where to find it** | Sidebar → Plan & decide → Financial Insights |
| **Web address** | `#/insights` |
| **Use it when** | At the start of a month to read how the last one went, or any time for a plain-language check-up |
| **Main actions** | Pick a month, filter observations by type, follow links |
| **Saves data?** | No, read-only |

## 2. Page layout

The page reads like a monthly report: a summary first, then chapters.

1. **Header:** a one-line summary of the month
2. **Month strip:** every month with transactions, each showing its savings rate ("19% saved", or "So far" for this month). The selected month is underlined
3. **Summary:** Income, Spent, Saved to goals and Left over (or Overspent), each compared with the month before, then **Where ₹X went** as one bar across the width
4. **Spending:** a sentence on how the month compares with your average, then **Month by month** (stacked bars) beside **Pace through the month** (running total against the month before)
5. **Savings:** a sentence on your savings rate against the 20% guide, then **Share of income saved** beside **Essentials and the rest**
6. **Categories against \<previous month\>:** a table of every category
7. **What stands out:** observations for the selected month, with the type switch (All / Spending / Savings / Upcoming, with counts)
8. **Disclaimer note**

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Month strip
- **What it does:** chooses the month the page reports on. It opens on your last complete month.
- **How to use it:** click a month, use **‹** / **›**, or focus the strip and press ← / →, Home or End. You can also click a month's bar in **Month by month** or **Share of income saved**. Only months with transactions are included; the current month is labelled "so far".

### 3.2 Header summary
- "October 2026: you saved **18% of income**." If you spent more than came in, it adds "and spent **₹X more than came in**" in lilac. For a past month it also repeats the top observation that needs attention.

### 3.3 Summary
| Figure | Shows | Underneath |
|---|---|---|
| **Income** | Income received in the month | Change vs the month before |
| **Spent** | All expenses | Change, and what share of income it was |
| **Saved to goals** | Transfers to goals, in deep periwinkle | Change, and your savings rate |
| **Left over** / **Overspent** | Income − spent − saved. "Stayed in your account", or "Came out of your balance" in lilac | Change |

- **Change** reads like "↑ ₹2,300 vs Sep". It's deep periwinkle when the change is good for you (more income, less spending, more saved) and lilac when it isn't. It says "Same as Sep" if nothing changed, or "No earlier month" for your first month.

### 3.4 Where ₹X went
- **What it does:** one bar showing how the month's income was used: **Essentials** (navy), **Non-essentials** (periwinkle), **Saved to goals** (pale periwinkle), and **Left over** (grey) or **Taken from balance** (lilac).
- **Below the bar:** each part with its amount and share. For the current month it says "So far, to \<today\>".

### 3.5 Spending, month by month
- **What it does:** one stacked bar per month: essentials (navy) under non-essentials (periwinkle), with income as a short line above. The selected month is in full colour; the others are faded.
- **The note above it** compares the month's spending with your average for the complete months.
- **How to use it:** hover over a month for its figures; click it to read that month.

### 3.5a Savings performance
- **What it does:** the share of income saved to goals each month, with a dashed line at a 20% guide.
- **The note above it** gives the month's rate, your average, and how far it is from the guide.

### 3.5b Essential vs non-essential
- **What it does:** the month's essentials and non-essentials as two bars, each with a tick at its average for up to three months before. Underneath: the usual amount and the difference (lilac when non-essentials ran more than ₹500 over), and what share of spending was non-essential.

### 3.6 Spending pace
- **What it does:** a running total of spending through the month, day by day. The selected month is a periwinkle line; the month before is a dashed grey line.
- **The note above it** says how far ahead or behind you are, for example "By day 31: ₹38,400, **₹2,100 more** than Sep at the same point". "More" is lilac, "less" is deep periwinkle.
- **How to use it:** hover over a day to see both running totals.

### 3.7 Categories against \<previous month\>
| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Category** | A filled dot for essentials, a hollow one for non-essentials |
| **Bar** | Thick periwinkle bar = this month. Thin grey line underneath = the month before |
| **\<Month\>** | This month's amount |
| **Change** | The difference in rupees. Lilac if it rose by ₹500 or more **and** 20% or more. Deep periwinkle if it fell |
| **3-month avg** | Average of the 3 months before (where you have them) |
| **Share** | Share of this month's spending |

- **Good to know:** the footer explains the dot and links to **Full spending analysis**.

### 3.8 What stands out
- Observations are worked out for the **selected month** against up to three months before it. Observations about today (what's due, goals, subscriptions, a typical month) appear only when you're reading your latest complete month. When the current month is selected, the observations come from the latest complete month, and the note says so.

- **What it does:** lists findings from your numbers, ranked by how much they need your attention (highest first).
- **Type switch:** **All**, **Spending**, **Savings** or **Upcoming**, each with a count.
- **Each observation shows:**

| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Figure** | A big number with a short note, for example "+45%, food & dining vs usual". Attention figures are lilac, good news is deep periwinkle and information stays navy |
| **Title** | The finding in one line |
| **Text** | The explanation in rupees |
| **Detail** | A smaller line with the numbers behind it (when available) |
| **Link** | Where to act on it |

- **Good to know:** observations always look at your **last complete month** and what's due now. They don't change when you pick a different month.

## 4. Step-by-step tasks

**How to review last month**
1. Open Financial Insights. It opens on your last complete month.
2. Read the header line and the four summary figures.
3. Check **Where ₹X went** and the **Categories** table to see what moved.
4. Read **What stands out** and follow the link on anything that needs attention.

**How to check this month so far**
1. Click **›** until the picker shows the current month ("so far").
2. Look at **Spending pace** to see whether you're ahead of last month at the same point.

**How to see only savings observations**
1. Click **Savings** in the type switch.

## 5. How the numbers are worked out

| Figure | Formula in words |
|---|---|
| **Income / Spent / Saved to goals** | Totals of income, expense and transfer transactions in the month |
| **Left over** | Income − spent − saved to goals |
| **Savings rate** | Saved to goals ÷ income |
| **Essentials / Non-essentials** | Expenses split by whether the category is marked essential |
| **Change** | This month's figure − the previous month's |
| **Spending pace** | Running total of expenses, day by day. Days after today are left blank |
| **3-month avg** | Average of the up to 3 months before, counting only months with transactions |
| **Observations** | The **last complete month** compared with the average of the up to **3 complete months before it**, plus your goals, upcoming expenses, typical month and money map |

- Everything uses the same transactions, goals and upcoming expenses as every other page.
- Observations need at least **2 complete months** of transactions.

## 8. Tips and FAQ

- **Why didn't the observations change when I picked another month?** They always read your last complete month and what's due now. The summary, charts and table follow the picker.
- **Why does Overview show fewer observations?** It shows the top 3 and leaves out the "due in 7 days" one, which its header already covers.
- **Why is the current month "so far"?** It isn't over yet, so its figures only run to today.
- **Do insights update on their own?** Yes. They're recalculated every time you open the page.
- **Are insights advice?** No. They describe your own numbers.
