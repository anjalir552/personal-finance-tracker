# Savings Goals

> **The question this page answers:** "Am I saving enough, soon enough?"

## 1. At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where to find it** | Sidebar → Plan & decide → Savings Goals |
| **Web address** | `#/goals` |
| **Use it when** | You start saving for something, add money to a goal, check progress or rethink your monthly amounts |
| **Main actions** | **New goal**, **Add money**, **Save plan**, edit, delete |
| **Saves data?** | Yes. Goals and their monthly amounts, plus transfer transactions when you add money |

## 2. Page layout

1. **Header:** whether every goal is on track (or which are behind), your monthly plan vs what's needed, and **New goal**
2. **Summary:** total saved and its share of all targets, then **Planned each month** (with what every deadline needs, in lilac when your plan is short), **Still to add this month** and **All goals done** (the month the last goal lands at your plan)
3. **Your goals:** one band per goal. On the left: name, status, saved of target, a progress bar with the % saved and what's left to go, and (when the goal is behind) how much more a month would finish it on time. In the middle: **Monthly plan**, **Deadline** with months left, and **Finishes**. On the right: **Add money**, edit and delete
4. **Trajectory and milestones:** a disclosure under each goal. It opens the goal's trajectory chart, one sentence on whether the plan lands before the deadline, and the four milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, Target) with "Reached" or the month each lands
5. **Reached goals:** a collapsed section (only if any goal is complete)
6. **Monthly plan:** a table of every goal (needed a month, your plan, when it finishes) with the totals for a typical month underneath, and **Reset** and **Save plan**
7. **Coming up:** what you've reached, then the next six milestones across all goals, soonest first
8. **Explanation note** about "Needed" and transfers

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Header summary
- When a goal is behind: "**Emergency fund is behind.** You plan ₹X a month; every deadline needs about ₹Y." The goal names are in lilac.
- When all are fine: "**Every goal is on track.** You plan ₹X a month, enough for each deadline."

### 3.2 Summary
| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Saved across N goals** | Total saved, and what share of all targets that is |
| **Planned each month** | The total of every goal's monthly plan. Underneath: what every deadline needs, in lilac when the plan is short |
| **Still to add this month** | What goals still need this cycle, or "Done" |
| **All goals done** | The month the last goal lands at your current plan |

### 3.3 Goal band
| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Name + status** | **On track**, **Behind plan**, **Past deadline** or **Reached** (**On track** is deep periwinkle; **Behind plan** and **Past deadline**, which need attention, are lilac) |
| **Note** | Optional text you added |
| **Amount** | "₹X of ₹target" |
| **Progress bar** | Periwinkle fill = saved, with "% saved" and "₹X to go" underneath |
| **Fix line** | Only when behind: "Add about ₹X a month to finish by \<deadline\>" |
| **Monthly plan** | Your monthly amount ("—" if none) |
| **Deadline** | The date, and "N months left" (or "Passed") |
| **Finishes** | The month the goal lands at your plan, in lilac if after the deadline |
| **Actions** | **Add money**, ✎ edit, 🗑 delete |
| **Trajectory and milestones** | Opens the chart and the milestone list below |

### 3.3a Trajectory chart
- **Solid periwinkle line:** what was saved at the end of each month since the goal was created (this month: up to today).
- **Dashed line:** where your monthly plan takes it, month by month, until the target. It is periwinkle when it lands by the deadline and lilac when it lands after.
- **Dashed horizontal line:** the target. **Dotted vertical line:** the deadline (lilac when you'd miss it).
- Hover over a month to see the figures.

### 3.4 Monthly plan
- **What it does:** lists every active goal with a monthly amount box, so you can rebalance them together.
- **How to use it:** type a new amount next to a goal. Under the box you'll see the month it would land and "on time" (deep periwinkle) or "after the deadline" (lilac). "Never" means ₹0 a month.
- **Totals:**

| Line | Shows |
|---|---|
| **A typical month after bills and essentials** | Income − recurring bills − everyday essentials |
| **Into goals with this plan** | The total of the amounts in the planner |
| **Left for everything else** | The difference. Lilac if it's less than your usual flexible spending |

  A note under the totals says whether that covers your usual eating out, shopping and plans, or how much less of it you'd need.
- **Buttons:** **Save plan** writes the new amounts to your goals. **Reset** puts back the saved ones. Both stay greyed out until you change something.

### 3.5 Coming up
- **What it does:** lists the next six milestones across all goals, soonest first: the month, the goal, and the milestone with its amount.
- **Good to know:** months are worked out from the amounts you plan to add, so they move when you save a new plan.

### 3.6 New / edit goal form
| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **What are you saving for?** | Required |
| **Target amount** | Required, above zero |
| **Target date** | Defaults to 180 days from today. Must be after today |
| **Already saved** *(optional)* | Money already put aside |
| **Plan to save each month** | Your monthly amount |
| **Note** *(optional)* | Why it matters |

- **Live suggestion box:** as you type, it says "To reach ₹X by \<date\>, save about **₹Y a month** for N months."
  - If your plan is lower, the box turns lilac and says when you'd actually get there.
  - If the suggestion is more than a typical month has spare, it warns that you may need to spend less elsewhere.
  - Click **Use ₹Y** to fill in the suggested amount.
  - If already saved ≥ target: "You've already saved enough for this."
- **Good to know:** when editing, "Already saved" shows the total including past transfers. Changing it adjusts the goal's starting amount, so your transfers stay intact.

### 3.7 Add money
- **What it does:** moves money from your balance into the goal, recorded as a **transfer** transaction.
- **How to use it:** click **Add money**. The amount is pre-filled with this cycle's remaining set-aside (or your monthly plan). Pick a date (today or earlier), then click **Add money**.
- **Good to know:** you can't add more than your current balance. When a goal is reached you'll see "\<Goal\> reached. Well done."

### 3.8 Delete a goal
- Confirm with **Delete goal**. Past transfers **stay** in Transactions.

### 3.9 Reached goals
- Completed goals move into a collapsible **Reached goals** section with a count. The track is fully filled and the pace line says "Reached ₹target". They have no **Add money** button but can still be edited or deleted.

## 4. Step-by-step tasks

**How to create a goal**
1. Click **New goal**.
2. Enter the name, target amount and target date.
3. Read the suggestion and click **Use ₹X** (or type your own amount).
4. Click **Create goal**.

**How to put money into a goal on payday**
1. Click **Add money** on the goal.
2. Keep the suggested amount or change it.
3. Click **Add money**. Your available balance goes down by the same amount.

**How to catch up on a goal that's behind**
1. In the **Monthly plan** table, raise the goal's amount until it says "on time".
2. Check **Left for everything else** still covers your usual spending. If not, lower another goal's amount.
3. Click **Save plan**.
4. Or click ✎ on the goal and move the **Target date** later.

## 5. How the numbers are worked out

| Figure | Formula in words |
|---|---|
| **Saved** | Already saved (start amount) + all transfers to this goal |
| **Remaining** | Target − saved |
| **Months left** | Calendar months from this month to the deadline month |
| **Needed per month** | Remaining ÷ months left (at least 1), rounded up to ₹100 on screen |
| **On track** | Your monthly plan ≥ needed per month |
| **Steady plan marker** | Days since you created the goal ÷ days from creation to the deadline, as a share of the track |
| **Ahead / behind schedule** | (Saved share − steady plan share) × target |
| **Milestone month** | Current month + (milestone amount − saved) ÷ monthly plan, rounded up |
| **Planner finish month** | Current month + remaining ÷ the amount in the box, rounded up |
| **A typical month after bills and essentials** | Monthly income − recurring bills − everyday average |
| **Added since payday** | Transfers to active goals since the last payday |
| **Still to add / set aside** | For each goal, the smaller of monthly plan and remaining, minus transfers since the last payday |

"Set aside" feeds the **Savings to set aside** layer of the money map.

## 6. Empty and special states

| Situation | What you see |
|---|---|
| No active goals | "No active goals" with **Create your first goal**. The summary, plan and milestones are hidden |
| Goal past its deadline and not reached | **Past deadline** badge, and Deadline shows "passed" |
| Monthly plan is ₹0 | "Plan" shows "—", milestones show amounts instead of months, and the planner says "Never". The goal still counts toward Needed |
| Nothing left this cycle | **Still to add this month** shows "Done" |
| On a phone | Everything stacks in one column; the plan table becomes one block per goal |

## 8. Tips and FAQ

- **Why did my free to spend drop after adding money?** The money left your available balance and moved into the goal.
- **The marker says I'm behind but the badge says On track. Which is right?** Both. You've saved less than a steady line would have by now, but your monthly plan is enough to catch up by the deadline.
- **Does typing in the planner change anything straight away?** No. Nothing is saved until you click **Save plan**.
- **Can I take money out of a goal?** There's no withdraw button. Delete the transfer in Transactions, or lower "Already saved" when editing.
- **Why are goals important for purchase checks?** This month's goal money is protected. A purchase that needs it gets the verdict "Only by pausing your savings".
