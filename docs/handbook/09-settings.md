# Settings

> **The question this page answers:** "What does Scopewise need to know about my money, and how do I manage my data?"

## 1. At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where to find it** | Sidebar → Settings (bottom), or click your name card |
| **Web address** | `#/settings` |
| **Use it when** | You first set up, your income or payday changes, your bank balance is out of sync, or you want to export or reset |
| **Main actions** | **Save money setup**, **Save**, **Export JSON**, **Clear all data**, **Restore example** |
| **Saves data?** | Yes |

## 2. Page layout

1. **Header**
2. **Money setup:** balance, income, payday, cushion
3. **About you:** name, city, life stage, default reminder, currency
4. **Your data:** export, start fresh, restore the example

Each section has an explanation on the left and the form on the right.

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Money setup
| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bank balance today** | Sets your current balance. Scopewise adjusts the opening balance so past transactions stay as they are |
| **Monthly income** | Salary, stipend or pocket money after tax. Used for typical-month advice and goal suggestions |
| **Income usually arrives on** | The 1st to the 28th of each month. Sets payday and the pay cycle |
| **Safety cushion** | The least you want left before payday. Purchase checks protect it |

- The left side shows live: "Right now: **₹X** free to spend until \<payday\>."
- **How to use it:** edit the fields, then click **Save money setup**.

### 3.2 About you
| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown in the sidebar, with your initials as an avatar |
| **City** *(optional)* | Shown under your name |
| **Where are you right now?** | College student / First job / Working a while. Shown in the sidebar |
| **Default reminder** | The reminder pre-selected for new upcoming expenses |
| **Currency** | Fixed: Indian rupee (₹) |

- **How to use it:** edit the fields, then click **Save**.

### 3.3 Export everything
- Downloads `scopewise-data.json` with all transactions, goals, upcoming expenses, decisions and settings.

### 3.4 Start fresh (Clear all data)
- Deletes everything from this browser after you confirm with **Clear everything**. It then takes you to Overview with the set-up checklist.

### 3.5 Restore the example
- Replaces your data with the sample profile (Aarav, a first-job designer in Pune) after you confirm with **Restore example**.

## 4. Step-by-step tasks

**How to set up with your own numbers**
1. **Export JSON** first if you want to keep anything.
2. Click **Clear all data**, then **Clear everything**.
3. Back in Settings, enter your bank balance, monthly income, payday and cushion, then click **Save money setup**.
4. Fill in **About you** and click **Save**.
5. Add your bills in **Upcoming Expenses** and your goals in **Savings Goals**. The Overview checklist goes away once you save the money setup, so add these from the sidebar.

**How to fix a balance that doesn't match your bank**
1. Type your real balance into **Bank balance today**.
2. Click **Save money setup**. Free to spend updates everywhere.

**How to change your payday**
1. Pick the new day in **Income usually arrives on**, then save. The pay cycle, days left and set-aside amounts all recalculate.

## 5. How the numbers are worked out

| Setting | Affects |
|---|---|
| Bank balance | Every balance figure and the money map |
| Monthly income | Typical month, "Wait until payday" and "Save towards it" advice, goal suggestions, the flexible-spending-gap insight |
| Payday | Days until payday, everyday-essentials estimate, which bills count as "before payday", goal set-aside |
| Safety cushion | Purchase verdicts and the "look for one under" amount |
| Default reminder | New upcoming expenses only |

## 8. Tips and FAQ

- **Will changing my balance delete transactions?** No. Only the opening balance moves.
- **Why can't I pick the 30th as payday?** Days 1 to 28 exist in every month. For a late-month payday, choose 28.
- **Is my data backed up?** Only if you export it. It lives in this browser alone.
- **Can I import a JSON export?** Not in this version. Export is for keeping a copy.
